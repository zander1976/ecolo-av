## The idea in one line

Every part sits on one of **nine levels** (below). All the sensing goes into **one world model**, and
**one boss** decides what to do. Each slot has a default and challengers, picked by
**one word**, not 36 switches.

## Problem

| What we see | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| 36 launch switches (`docs/switches.md`) | no one knows which mix is "the car" |
| The lane driver is a rival driver | lanes take turns with Nav2 through twist_mux; they never guide it |
| Some sensing never reaches the planner | the detector's labels, the voxels (a trial) |
| Three "what next" deciders | Nav2's trees, our navigator, the behaviour planner: who is in charge? |
| The car wiggles under Nav2 (TODO 124) | nothing holds it to a lane; the path floats on a wide free road |

## The levels

Ben, 13 Nov: routing "will be there no matter what. If someone has a destination then the car
should know that. That is separate from the drivers that actually drive the car."

```text
 ALWAYS ON, whoever drives
 1 Routing        which roads           campus router (road graph)
 2 Localization   where am I            EKF: GPS, IMU, wheels (+ NDT)
 3 Perception     what's around         lidar, radar, camera boxes, lane lines
 4 World model    where may I go        costmap: walls + soft lanes

 THE "NAV" DRIVER: one of several drivers
 5 Behaviour      the rules             stop, wait, pass, speed cap (a state machine)
 6 Path           the exact line        lattice, diffusion, Hybrid-A*
 7 Follow+avoid   steer along it        MPPI (avoids), RPP, MPC (follow only)

 PICK ONE DRIVER: twist_mux, by priority
    joystick > RC > keyboard > tests > scripted > buttons > replay > point (GPS) > nav > ai

 ALWAYS LAST
 8 Ego            what the car can do   speed + turn -> steering angle; limits, lag
 9 Safety         may it move at all    e-stop, geofence, stop sign, watchdog, firmware
```

Levels 1-4 serve every driver and the locks. Levels 5-7 are one driver, `nav`. Diffusion
draws the path (level 6); it doesn't steer. Ego is not a state machine: it translates and limits.

## Four rules

| # | Rule | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One world model** | Every sensor and map writes into one layered costmap and one pose. Planners and trackers read only those. A new idea becomes a layer, not a new driver |
| 2 | **One boss** | The behaviour planner alone decides *what*: go, stop, wait, pass. Path and steering decide *how* |
| 3 | **Slots, not switches** | Each box has a default and challengers. `stack:=stock`, `stack:=ours`, `stack:=car` pick a whole set |
| 4 | **One scorecard** | Every challenger is scored on the same campus routes and the same recordings (`measure`) |

## From 36 switches to 8

Ben, 13 Nov: "Thats to many switches, we want to reduce that for sure." One switch per level,
one word for the whole set. Options are cheap; switches are not. No algorithm is deleted.

| Switch | Options | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| `stack:=` | `stock`, `ours`, `car` | sets every switch below at once |
| `pose:=` | `ekf`, `ndt`, `kiss`, `amcl` | `ndt`, `ndt_ekf`, `kiss_icp`, `kiss_ekf`, `amcl` |
| `costmap:=` | `nav2`, `ours`; `layers:=voxels,lanes` | `costmap`, `voxels` |
| `boss:=` | `nav2`, `ours`, `behaviour` | `navigator`, `behaviour` |
| `path:=` | `lattice`, `lattice_rs`, `hybrid`, `diffusion`, `smac2d`, `navfn`, `theta_star` | `planner` (both meanings) |
| `steer:=` | `mppi`, `mppi_lag`, `mpc_lag`, `rpp`, `stanley`, `pure_pursuit`, `lqr`, `mpc` | `controller`, `tracker` |
| `world:=`, `simulator:=` | unchanged | — |

The test knobs (`lag_scale`, `sim_speed`, `geofence`, `lidar`) stay, on their own list.
The old names keep working for a release, with a warning, so no one's commands break at once.

## First piece: the lane layer

Lane lines are a **cost, not a wall**. Crossing is allowed, but only when staying is worse.

```text
 cost   kerb     edge line          centre line                       edge line    kerb
 wall   ███                                                                        ███
 high   ███      ▓▓                                                        ▓▓      ███
 mid    ███                         ▒▒▒                                            ███
 low    ███      ░░░░░ left lane (oncoming) ░░░░                                   ███
 none   ███                                    right lane: free                    ███
```

| Situation | What the car does |
|---|---|
| Road clear | the right lane is cheapest: it stays there, centred |
| Right lane blocked | the block is a wall; going round through the left lane costs less than never arriving |
| The boss says "no passing here" | it raises the left lane's cost |

| Source | Gives |
|---|---|
| `maps/campus/lanes.yaml` (OpenStreetMap) | every road's centre line, width, one-way. Today: only for scoring |
| the lane finder (camera) | the real lines, live; corrects the map |

**Step 1, stock:** a small Python node turns `lanes.yaml` into a mask; Nav2's own keepout filter
reads it (it takes in-between costs, not only walls: `keepout_filter.cpp`). No Nav2 change.
**Step 2, ours:** a `lanes` layer in the Rust costmap that also takes the camera's lines.
**Score:** `measure lane` on campus roads, with and without; how often it crosses; the wiggle.

## Done means

| Check | Pass |
